Who is AmanData?

AmanData is a Nigerian VTU and digital payment platform. We help individuals, resellers and businesses buy affordable data bundles, recharge airtime on every network, pay for prepaid electricity tokens, renew cable TV subscriptions and settle bills, all from one simple and secure account.

Our name carries our promise. "Aman" stands for trust, safety and peace of mind, and that is how we treat every wallet funding, every transaction and every customer who chooses us.

From our office in Gusau, we serve customers across Nigeria with fast delivery, fair prices and friendly support available every day of the week.
